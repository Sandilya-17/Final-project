## Models and tools
1. Devlin et al. (2019). BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding. NAACL-HLT.
2. Conneau et al. (2020). Unsupervised Cross-lingual Representation Learning at Scale (XLM-R). ACL.
3. Doddapaneni et al. (2023). Towards Leaving No Indic Language Behind: Building Monolingual Corpora, Benchmark and Models for Indic Languages (IndicBERT v2, IndicXTREME). ACL.
4. Kakwani et al. (2020). IndicNLPSuite: Monolingual Corpora, Evaluation Benchmarks and Pre-trained Multilingual Language Models for Indian Languages (IndicGLUE, IndicBERT). Findings of EMNLP.
5. Khanuja et al. (2021). MuRIL: Multilingual Representations for Indian Languages. arXiv.
6. Wolf et al. (2020). Transformers: State-of-the-Art Natural Language Processing. EMNLP (System Demonstrations).

9. Joshi et al. (2020). The State and Fate of Linguistic Diversity and Inclusion in the NLP World. ACL.
